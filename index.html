Calculation notes

  • Daily crop water use: ETc = ETo × Kc.
  • Plant-available water: root depth × available water capacity.
  • Allowable depletion: plant-available water × allowable depletion fraction × wetted-volume fraction.
  • Gross applied depth: low-quarter net depth ÷ DU ÷ (1 − runoff fraction); runtime is gross depth ÷ precipitation rate.
  • When monthly ETo is selected, values are linearly interpolated between monthly midpoints. When a daily CSV is selected, its ETo value is used directly for each date. Monthly precipitation is placed on evenly spaced planning days and limited to water that can refill the root zone.
